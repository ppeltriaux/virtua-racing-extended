# Phase 22 recon: the car on banked curves, the in-race map (1 Nov 2026)

Pascal's playtest (Surfers Road, Diablo Canyon): on curves tilted sideways the car does not bank left/right and goes
under the drawn road (up/down is fine); the in-race map looks inverted and does not show where the car is.

## 1. Banking: the collision is capped, the car rolls about half

- The collision's side tilt is the Saturn slice normal's lateral slope (`profile.arc_height`, Phase 16), clamped to
  `MAX_CAMBER_UNITS` = 1.3 units against the profile at any lateral: at the AI lines (±8) at most 9.2°.
- Drawn banking (the ground mesh 8 units either side of the centreline, `bank.py`): Surfers Road 48 points over 8°
  (max 12.8°), Surfers Oval 21 (12.7°), Diablo Canyon 5 (9.1°), Amazon 1 (8.4°), the rest under 8°.
- The player's roll (`+0x2e`, attitude log every 5 frames, autopilot, Surfers Road): on drawn banks over 6° the roll
  follows the bank's sign (correlation −0.85, negative = right side up) but averages 4.9° against 9.3° drawn; on the
  12.6-12.8° banking it is 5.4-6.0°, and at points 131-133 0°. A car rolled 5° on a 13° road sinks its high side
  into the drawn surface.
- **Spike** (`MAX_CAMBER_UNITS` 3.0, Surfers Road alone): the collision tilts 12.7-12.8° on those curves and the roll
  is 12.2-14.2° (correlation −0.99, mean 10.3° against 9.4° drawn); no 0° spot.

## 2. The in-race map: the outline is not where the car is

- Snapshots at known frames (Surfers Road, `map_run.py`): the outline has the course's shape but the cross (the car,
  always at screen centre) is not on it; near one end the outline is a tangle of several lines (the lap seam?).
- Our centreline transformed by the documented map convention (car-centred, heading up) does not overlay the
  drawn outline, mirrored or not; free fits (scale, rotation, mirror, translation) are weak and inconsistent.
- The minimap entity (handler `0xffcb59`, kept from the host at the origin, the same object list on all three
  originals) only manages the car markers. The outline is generated by the TGP at course load (`poly 0x101ad4` on
  Big Forest's slot, Phase 14): the command and the data it reads are still to find.

## 3. Split (as 18a/18b)

- **22a**: the banking — raise the side-tilt cap so the collision follows the drawn banking; gates on the drawn vs
  collision tilt and on the player's roll in MAME.
- **22b**: the map — find the TGP outline build and what our road data feeds it.
