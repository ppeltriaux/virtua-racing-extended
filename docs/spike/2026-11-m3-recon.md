# Spike log: recon for M3 (Nov 2026, before Phase 6)

What a new course needs beyond collision data, measured on the arcade ROM set
and the Saturn disc before planning Phase 6. Numbers are arcade units unless
said otherwise.

## 1. The tile transform used since Phase 4 is half a turn off

`convert.geometry.course_quads` places Saturn tiles with the same
`to_arcade` (negate x and z, scale 11.7) as the road slices. The road side is
right: Phase 5's slices matched the arcade lanes within 3 units and the car
drove on them. The tiles are not:

| Saturn Big Forest tiles vs arcade tile geometry (64-unit occupancy cells) | overlap | Jaccard |
|---|---|---|
| as placed by `course_quads` | 158 / 235 | 0.58 |
| rotated a further 180° (x and z negated again) | 194 / 235 | **0.82** |
| x mirrored only / z mirrored only | 158 / 160 | 0.58 / 0.59 |

On courses that are not nearly symmetric it is plain: Alpine's road spans
x −170..868, z −475..961 while its tiles land at x −877..182, z −965..483
(Diablo Canyon and Pacific Coast the same). The rendered overlay
(`bf_group0_vs_slices.png` in the session scratchpad) shows the Saturn ground,
grey road surface included, as the mirror image of the slice outlines. This
also explains Phase 4's "pit buildings gone" and most of "ground covers the
road". **Fix first in Phase 6, then re-check the Phase 4 build.**

## 2. What draws the arcade road

- Shifting 40 road-line centreline points of Big Forest by +40 in x in the
  data bank does **not** move the drawn road (snapshots at race start + 200,
  320, 440 frames show it straight). The car is instead treated as off the
  road: 84 km/h and dust where the original does 215. So the **road surface is
  part of the tile objects**, and the road lines drive lap position, the
  grass penalty and the AI, not the picture.
- A race frame's display list (walker now matches MAME's `draw_direct`,
  which also stops at command 0x0f) holds 45 objects: the 16 cars and the 12
  visible tile cells. There are no separate road objects.
- Consequence for a converted course: the road picture comes from the Saturn
  ground tiles (which carry the grey road faces at y 0–1 on Big Forest), the
  collision from Phase 5, and the lap logic from generated road lines.

## 3. The tile-cell rule

The V60 keeps the visible cell list at `0x501cc0` (count, then cell indices;
11–13 cells). Logged against the car position through a Big Forest race, the
car's own cell follows 128-unit cells: z boundaries at ≈ −243, −113, 15,
142, 266, 394, 527 (spacing 124–133, i.e. 128 with update lag), x cell 6 for
x −230..−120. Phase 4's centroid fit (118 × 125 from −943 / −1003) matched
873 / 916 frames; 128-unit cells from −1024 (= collision cell >> 3) are the
natural rule and fit z as well; the car's x range is too narrow to separate
the two. Settle it in Phase 6 from the code that writes `0x501cc0`.

## 4. Saturn ordering and heights

- Slices are stored in lap-ordered **runs**: 316 / 430 consecutive indices are
  adjacent (< 2 Saturn units) on Big Forest, 371 / 527 on Alpine; the runs are
  joined by jumps. Big Forest slice 0 sits on the start straight at
  (−185, −497), slice 1 at (−185, −484): index order inside a run is the
  direction of travel (the arcade drives +z there). Alpine slice 0 is at
  (−141, 614) → slice 1 (−141, 626).
- The 32-row sections 26 / 41 / 43 (43 rows on Alpine, 41 Amazon, 57 / 48 Bay
  Bridge) are `(cos, sin, x, z, w, flag)` with positions up to 400 units from
  the road: trackside cameras, not AI lines. Section 28 (3–4 rows, last word
  ≈ 2.7) likewise. Sections 31–34 are two 16.16 values and two small ints.
- Segment word 18 is 0 on every course; the surface normal is the only slope
  data. The Saturn ground tiles (group 0) carry the road faces, so once the
  tile transform is fixed the road height comes from the mesh under each
  slice; integrating the normals along the ordered lap is the fallback.
- Neither plane identity nor exactly shared corners chain the slices (seams);
  ordering must tolerate ~0.3-unit gaps or use the runs above.

## 5. Arcade road tables (for the generator)

- Centreline spacing: median 10.1 units (min 5.3, max 13.8); heading word =
  angle in 1/16384 turn in the high half; `link = segment << 16 | small`
  (low half mostly 0, else −4..20).
- Four AI lines, each the same point count as the centreline, lateral offsets
  within ±13 (medians −2..+1): racing lines, not constant offsets.
- Segments: 1092 for 338 points on Big Forest (1 per point on 202 points, 2 on
  33, 8 on 103); flags 1 except 66 on the first 34 segments (road points
  0–33, the pit/grid zone). Bay Bridge uses flags 1, 2, 4, 16, 129 (unknown).
- Road point 0 is ~130 units before the grid; the player starts at road point
  ~13 (z −270) in the left lane.

## 6. Per-course things the swap build will inherit from Big Forest

- Boot ROM `0xfdb90b` (RAM `0x50197c`): 42 eight-byte records
  `(x, y, byte, 0x50xx/0x40xx)` — the HUD minimap polyline (screen coordinates).
- RAM `0x50195c` → `0xfcf199` and `0x501968` → `0xfcd66d`: per-course data of
  unknown format (not clean code, not floats). The car table Phase 2 named at
  `0xfcd395` is not a 32-byte-per-car table either.
- Graphics uploads (backdrop, palette) and the undecoded bank table at
  +0x8eb8.
